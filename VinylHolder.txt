Vinyl Holder
Idea:
Media player that scans an NFC tag inside a vinyl case, and begins playing that album or playlist.

Design thoughts:
Media player: Offline, custom speakers, audio files stored on SD card so must be updated manually.

Spotify API: must have good wifi connection, use echo dot, can play albums on spotify but not playlists.

Design to imitate 1960s-1970s magazine or vinyl holders.
